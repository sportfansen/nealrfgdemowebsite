Section 03 / Procedure

Detailing, step by step

The paint is machine polished panel by panel to bring back the depth and gloss, as on the Range Rover in the video.

  1. Machine Polishing

    Panel by panel, to bring back the depth and gloss.

  2. Engine Bay & Wheels

    Engine bay wiped down and dressed; wheels, tyres and arches cleaned.

  3. Seats & Dashboard

    Seats, dashboard, door cards and trim cleaned and treated.

  4. Every Corner

    Vents, switches, screens and console gaps worked through by hand.

  5. Ready To Show

    Back on the showroom floor under the DBI sign, ready to collect.
